Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · See also our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines

1. Introduction

Welcome to Connectango!

Connectango is a community platform designed to help Argentine tango dancers connect, share rides, find accommodations, and coordinate travel around tango events.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

By using Connectango, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Connectango, we may collect the following information:

Account and profile information

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your Facebook profile link, if provided
  • Other information you voluntarily add to your profile

Event and travel information

  • Events you join through Connectango
  • Travel dates and approximate arrival or departure times
  • Ride-sharing requests and offers
  • Accommodation and hosting preferences
  • Information you choose to share with other participants

Technical information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the platform, such as authentication records, IP addresses, and application error information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect you with other tango dancers
  • Facilitate ride sharing, room sharing, and hosting arrangements
  • Help event organizers coordinate participating dancers
  • Send essential notifications about your account and activities
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the platform
  • Improve the user experience

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information Shared With Other Users

Connectango is designed around community connections. Certain information may be visible to other registered users, depending on the features you use.

This may include:

  • Your name and profile information
  • Events you are participating in
  • Ride-sharing or accommodation requests
  • Hosting availability and preferences
  • Travel details you choose to share

Information is shared to facilitate connections and coordination.

We encourage you not to share sensitive personal information, such as your exact home address, until you feel comfortable with the person you are communicating with.

5. Information Shared With Event Organizers

Event organizers may have access to certain information about participants associated with their events, including names, participation status, and relevant ride-sharing or accommodation activities.

This information is provided to help organizers coordinate their events and support participants.

6. Third-Party Services

Connectango uses third-party services to operate the platform, which may include:

  • Supabase, for authentication and secure data storage
  • Resend, for transactional emails such as account verification
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, for protection against automated abuse

These services may process limited personal or technical information as necessary to provide their functionality, subject to their respective privacy practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including authentication controls, database access restrictions, and encrypted communications.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Connectango services, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Some information may be retained temporarily where necessary for security, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may:

  • Update your profile information
  • Choose which ride-sharing, hosting, or accommodation opportunities to participate in
  • Avoid sharing optional personal information
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information

10. Community Safety

Connectango helps dancers connect but does not independently verify the identity, reliability, or intentions of every participant.

Users are responsible for exercising appropriate judgment when arranging transportation, accommodation, or in-person meetings.

We encourage all participants to communicate clearly, respect boundaries, and prioritize personal safety.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Connectango evolves.

When changes are made, we will update the date at the top of this document. For significant changes, we may provide additional notice through the app or by email.
