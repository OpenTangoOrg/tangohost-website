Getting started with the app
Thanks for helping us test! Follow these steps to get set up before the event.
What we're testing
This app is designed to help tango travelers:
  • Find someone to share a ride with, when you're traveling at the same time.
  • Find someone to share a hotel room or Airbnb.
  • Find a local host, or host a visiting dancer.
  • Find a partner to register for an event that requires couples registration.
For this pilot:
Our main focus is testing ride sharing, so we'd love for everyone to try creating rides, finding travel companions, and sending requests.
You can also explore room sharing if you'd like, although we'll mostly be pretending, since most of you have probably already arranged your accommodations for this event!
The hosting and registration partner features are part of our roadmap, but they're not implemented yet in this pilot. You may see hosting-related screens or buttons, but be aware that these are not connected to our database yet.

Enter your ride from the airport to the venue
In the "My Trips" screen, tap the + button at the bottom of the screen, and select "Find Ride". Enter the details for your ride (O'Hare Airport to Epiphany Center of the Arts, maybe), date and departing time.

At any time you can check here, the app will show you which other attendees are looking for a ride around the same time. You can invite them to share a Taxi/Uber, or you may receive an invitation. Invitations also show in your activity menu — the rightmost option in the bottom bar of the "My Trips" screen.

Add your Facebook profile link
Go to the Facebook app, go to your Profile, go to the 3 dots icon in the upper right corner, locate "Copy profile link", and click it. Then in this app, go to your account settings, open "Personal Info", and paste the link to your Facebook profile. When you match with someone for a ride or a room, you can check each other's Facebook profile before deciding whether to connect. If you need to communicate, you can also message each other through Facebook Messenger.
