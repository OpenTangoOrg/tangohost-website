About us

Built by dancers, for dancers.

Connectango comes from a team of longtime Argentine tango dancers who think more people should get to experience traveling for tango. We pair years of event-organizing experience with software engineering to make travel easier, more affordable, and more social, while giving back to a community that has given us a great deal. As a nonprofit project, our goal is to keep Connectango free for the tango community.
