About us

Built by dancers, for dancers.

TangoHost comes from a team of longtime Argentine tango dancers who think more people should get to experience traveling for tango. We pair years of event-organizing experience with software engineering to make travel easier and more affordable — and to give something back to a community that has given us a great deal.
