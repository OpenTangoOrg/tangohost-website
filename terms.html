Community Guidelines & Legal Disclaimer

For ride-share and lodging-share coordination among Argentine tango event attendees

Part 1: Community Guidelines

Our Purpose

TangoHost exists to help the tango community — dancers traveling to milongas, marathons, encuentros, and festivals — coordinate shared rides and spare rooms. This only works if people treat each other with the same respect and care expected on the dance floor.

1. Respect and Conduct

  • Treat every host, driver, passenger, and guest with courtesy, regardless of dance level, nationality, age, or how long they've been in the community.
  • No harassment, unwanted advances, discriminatory remarks, or predatory behavior — on the platform, in a car, or in a shared home. Tango's culture of physical closeness on the dance floor does not extend to ride-share or lodging arrangements; consent and personal boundaries apply fully here.
  • No solicitation of dance favors, private lessons, or any other exchange as a condition of a ride or room.

2. Ride-Sharing Etiquette

  • Cost-sharing, not a taxi service. Arrangements should be for splitting fuel, tolls, and travel costs among people going to the same event — not run as a for-profit transport business, unless you hold the appropriate commercial license required in your jurisdiction.
  • Be honest about departure times, luggage space, detours, and smoking/pet preferences before confirming.
  • Confirm or cancel promptly. Last-minute cancellations strand people far from home — give as much notice as possible.
  • Drivers: hold a valid license and appropriate insurance for carrying passengers. Passengers: wear seatbelts and follow the driver's vehicle rules.

3. Lodging-Sharing Etiquette

  • Hosts should accurately describe the space (private room vs. shared floor, bedding, bathroom access, distance to the venue) and any house rules (quiet hours, guests, shoes, pets, cooking) before a guest commits.
  • Guests should respect the host's home, house rules, and privacy, and communicate arrival/departure times clearly.
  • Practice dancing, playing music, or hosting after-milonga gatherings in a shared home should be discussed and agreed to in advance, not assumed.
  • No use of a listed lodging space for any purpose other than what was agreed (e.g., subletting, parties not disclosed to the host).

4. Honesty and Identity

  • Use your real name and an accurate profile. Impersonation or fake profiles are grounds for removal.
  • Reviews and ratings should reflect genuine experiences. Do not offer or accept payment, discounts, or favors in exchange for a review.

5. Zero-Tolerance Behaviors

The following result in immediate suspension pending review:

  • Sexual harassment, assault, or unwanted physical contact
  • Threats, violence, or intimidation
  • Discrimination based on protected characteristics
  • Theft, property damage, or fraud
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Any illegal activity conducted through or facilitated by the platform

6. Reporting

If something goes wrong, report it through the in-app report function or [support contact]. In an emergency, contact local emergency services first (911 in Argentina, or the local equivalent at your event's location) — the platform is not a substitute for emergency response.

7. Consequences

Violations may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the platform, at TangoHost's discretion. Serious violations (safety threats, illegal conduct) may be reported to local authorities.
