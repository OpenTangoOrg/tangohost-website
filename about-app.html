Connectango

Travel further, together.

Connectango connects dancers whose journeys overlap, so the practical parts of a trip — the ride, the room — get easier to sort out.

Why we built it

Travel more, spend less

A shared taxi and a shared room turn a trip you were weighing into a trip you take.

Arrive knowing someone

Reconnect with old friends, meet new ones — the ride from the airport is where it starts.

Travel changes you

New cities, new floors, new people. Going further widens what you think is possible.

What you can do

Share a ride

Post your airport arrival, see who lands near your time, split the taxi.

Share a room

Offer a spare bed or find one — hotel or Airbnb, for the nights you need.

Host, or find a host

Open your home to a visiting dancer, or stay with a local when you travel.

Offer seats in your car

Driving or renting a car? Post your empty seats and pick up dancers along the way.

Organizer shuttles

Running a shuttle for your event? Post it so guests can find and book a seat.

Find a registration partner

For events that require couples registration, find someone to sign up with.

Controlled visibility

Only see what you need to see.

Coordinating travel shouldn't mean broadcasting your plans to a whole group chat.

You're visible to the right people

Only the people looking for the same thing, at the same time, see that you're looking too.

Hosts stay in control

You decide who sees your details, and when. Your address isn't a public listing.

Tap to answer

Accept or decline in one tap. Less typing, fewer threads to keep up with.

Say no without explaining

Declining is a button, not a conversation. No reason required, no awkwardness.

Going somewhere soon?

Add your trip and see who else is on their way.
