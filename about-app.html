Connectango

Travel more, together.

Every tango dancer knows the feeling: you've found the perfect festival or milonga in another city, but you're stuck figuring out where to stay, how to get there, and who you can trust. Connectango is a community-built home for tango dancers around the world to share housing, rides, and local knowledge. Every member is vetted by their peers and reviewed by the people they've hosted, driven, or helped, so you can connect with confidence. And it's donation-supported, so it's free for everyone. No fees, no barriers, just dancers helping dancers.

Why we built it

Traveling for tango is wonderful, but it can get expensive. Hotels, rental cars, and airport transfers add up quickly, especially when you're traveling alone.

Meanwhile, other dancers may be making similar plans, looking for someone to share a ride or a room. Some of them might even be people you've danced with before! And you might even find a local dancer willing to offer you a spare room for your stay.

We created Connectango to make these connections easier, so more people can travel, dance, and be part of the tango community.

A smarter, more efficient way to connect

Finding people to share travel arrangements with shouldn't mean posting your plans to an entire Facebook group and hoping the right person sees them.

With Connectango, you can search for dancers whose plans match yours and get notified when new matches appear.

You decide what to share, who to connect with, and which arrangements feel right for you.

What you can do

  • Share an airport ride

    Arriving around the same time as other dancers? Find someone to share a taxi or Uber.

  • Share a room

    Looking for a roommate for a festival? Find someone to split a hotel room or Airbnb with.

  • Host or find a host

    Have a spare room? Offer hospitality to a visiting dancer. Traveling somewhere new? Connect with local dancers who are open to hosting.

  • Share a car ride

    Driving to an event or renting a car? Offer your empty seats, or find someone heading in the same direction.

  • Find an event shuttle

    Event organizers can share shuttle options so dancers can find transportation more easily.

  • Find a registration partner

    Attending an event that requires couples registration? Connect with someone looking for a partner.
