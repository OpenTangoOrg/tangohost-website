# Personal Safety

**Connect with confidence. Share with care.**

Connectango brings tango dancers together to share rides, accommodations, and hospitality, making tango travel more affordable, accessible, and connected.

We believe in the generosity and goodwill of the tango community. Many of us have shared wonderful experiences with people we've met through tango.

At the same time, sharing a car, a room, or a home involves personal risks. Your safety and comfort should always come first.

## Your safety, your choice

Connectango helps members find and connect with other dancers, but we do not provide transportation or accommodations ourselves.

Every member is responsible for deciding whom they feel comfortable traveling with, sharing accommodations with, or hosting.

You are always free to decline a request, withdraw an invitation, or cancel an arrangement if you feel uncomfortable.

**You never owe anyone a ride, a place to stay, or an explanation for choosing not to participate.**

## Community trust and verification

Connectango uses community connections and event participation to help members make more informed decisions about whom they connect with.

**Trusted Members**

Our trust network is based on invitations between members of the tango community. Trusted Members can invite others into the trusted network.

This reflects a community connection, not a formal assessment of someone's character or safety.

**Verified Attendees**

Members who enter a valid event code receive a Verified Attendee badge for that event.

This indicates that they have access to the event's code. It does not mean that Connectango has independently confirmed their identity, attendance, or background.

**What these designations mean**

Neither Trusted Member status nor a Verified Attendee badge is a background check, personal endorsement by Connectango, or guarantee of someone's reliability, conduct, or safety.

Connectango does not conduct criminal background checks on members.

We encourage all members to use their own judgment when making arrangements, regardless of another member's status.

## Get to know the people you connect with

Before making arrangements, we encourage you to:

- Review the other person's profile and available community connections.
- Reach out through shared contacts when possible.
- Communicate directly before confirming plans.
- Discuss expectations, logistics, and any concerns ahead of time.
- Trust your instincts. If something doesn't feel right, it's okay to say no.

## Sharing rides safely

If you're arranging a carpool or taxi share:

- Agree on the pickup location, destination, timing, and costs beforehand.
- Make sure everyone understands the transportation arrangements.
- For private carpools, drivers should be properly licensed, insured, and operating a roadworthy vehicle.
- Wear a seat belt and follow applicable local laws.
- Consider sharing your itinerary with someone you trust.
- Never feel obligated to enter or remain in a vehicle if you feel unsafe.

Connectango does not screen drivers, inspect vehicles, or verify insurance coverage.

## Sharing rooms safely

When sharing a hotel room, vacation rental, or other accommodation:

- Confirm the location, dates, sleeping arrangements, and costs in advance.
- Discuss expectations about privacy, guests, shared spaces, and quiet hours.
- Make sure everyone understands cancellation and payment arrangements.
- Avoid sending money through unfamiliar or suspicious payment methods.
- Respect personal boundaries and shared spaces.
- Consider having a backup accommodation plan if an arrangement doesn't work out.

Connectango does not inspect accommodations or verify the accuracy of listings.

## Hosting and staying with local dancers

Hospitality can be one of the most rewarding parts of the tango community. It also requires mutual trust and consideration.

**If you're hosting:**

- Share relevant details about your home and sleeping arrangements before accepting a guest.
- Communicate your house rules and expectations.
- Protect your personal information and home address until you are comfortable sharing them.
- Remember that you have the right to decline a request.

**If you're staying with someone:**

- Make sure you understand where you'll be staying and what is being offered.
- Respect your host's home, privacy, and boundaries.
- Let someone you trust know where you're staying.
- Have an alternative plan if you need to leave.

Both hosts and guests should feel comfortable communicating their needs and boundaries.

## Protect your personal information

Connectango is designed to help you connect without making all your travel plans and contact information public.

Even so, we encourage you to use care when sharing personal details.

- Share phone numbers, home addresses, and other sensitive information only when necessary.
- Be mindful of what you share through external services such as WhatsApp or Facebook.
- Never share passwords, financial credentials, or identification documents with other members.
- Be cautious of unexpected payment requests or suspicious links.

For more information about how Connectango handles personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.

## Respect, consent, and boundaries

Connectango is built on mutual respect.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexual misconduct, coercion, and other abusive behavior are not tolerated.

Respect other members' decisions, including when someone declines an invitation, cancels an arrangement, or asks to end communication.

Consent and personal boundaries matter, whether you're sharing a dance, a ride, a room, or a home.

Please also review our Community Guidelines.

## Reporting safety concerns

If you experience or witness harassment, threatening behavior, fraud, or other safety concerns involving a Connectango member or arrangement, please contact us at **[safety email address]**.

When reporting an incident, include any relevant details you're comfortable sharing, such as the people involved, the arrangement, and what happened.

Connectango administrators can review reports and restrict or block accounts when appropriate.

**Connectango is a free, volunteer-run platform.** We do not have dedicated safety staff or round-the-clock monitoring. Although we take reports seriously and aim to review them as resources permit, we cannot guarantee a response within a specific timeframe or immediate action.

We cannot guarantee the behavior of members or independently verify every report.

**If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.** Do not rely on Connectango for urgent assistance.

## Understanding the risks

Connectango is a community coordination platform. We facilitate introductions and arrangements between independent members, but we do not operate, supervise, or guarantee the transportation, accommodations, or hospitality offered by members.

Participating in arrangements made through Connectango involves risks, including injury, accidents, theft, harassment, assault, property loss, and other harm.

Members are responsible for assessing these risks and making their own decisions about participating.

Connectango cannot guarantee the identity, conduct, intentions, or safety of its members.

Your use of Connectango is also governed by our Terms of Service, which describe the platform's role, members' responsibilities, and applicable limitations of liability, subject to applicable law.

## A note about our pilot

Connectango is currently being tested with a limited group of tango community members.
