# Connectango Community Guidelines

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## Welcome to Connectango!

Connectango brings Argentine tango dancers together to share rides, accommodations, and travel experiences.

Our community is built on trust, generosity, and mutual respect. Whether you're offering someone a ride, welcoming a guest into your home, or sharing a hotel room, a little thoughtfulness goes a long way.

These guidelines help keep Connectango a safe, welcoming, and enjoyable place for everyone.

They complement our **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**, which provide additional information about your responsibilities and how the platform operates.

## 1. Respect Each Other

Treat everyone with courtesy and consideration, regardless of dance experience, age, nationality, gender, or background.

- Respect personal boundaries and consent.
- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and unwanted advances are not tolerated.
- Never pressure someone into accepting an invitation, ride, or accommodation.
- Do not request dances, private lessons, romantic attention, or personal favors in exchange for a ride or room.
- Respect someone's decision to decline an offer or cancel an arrangement.

**Remember: The closeness we share on the dance floor does not imply intimacy or familiarity outside of it. Personal boundaries matter everywhere.**

## 2. Sharing Rides

Sharing a ride can make tango travel more affordable, convenient, and enjoyable.

Whether you're splitting a taxi or offering a seat in your own car, please:

- Be accurate about departure times, pickup locations, luggage space, and planned detours.
- Discuss costs and payment arrangements before confirming.
- Communicate preferences concerning smoking, pets, or other relevant conditions.
- Arrive on time and notify others promptly of changes.
- Cancel as early as possible if your plans change.

**If you're driving:**

- Hold a valid driver's license and appropriate insurance.
- Ensure your vehicle is roadworthy and complies with applicable laws.
- Drive responsibly and never under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Make sure passengers have access to appropriate seatbelts.

**If you're a passenger:**

- Respect the driver's vehicle and reasonable rules.
- Wear your seatbelt.
- Be considerate of other passengers.

Ride sharing should comply with applicable transportation laws, including any rules governing cost sharing or commercial transportation.

## 3. Sharing Accommodations

Opening your home to another dancer, or sharing accommodations while traveling, can be a wonderful way to build friendships across the tango community.

Clear expectations help everyone feel comfortable.

**If you're hosting:**

- Describe the accommodation honestly, including sleeping arrangements, privacy, bathroom access, and relevant amenities.
- Communicate house rules, including expectations about noise, guests, pets, smoking, and use of shared spaces.
- Be clear about arrival and departure times.
- Respect your guest's privacy and personal boundaries.
- Make sure you are permitted to host guests under applicable laws and property rules.

**If you're a guest:**

- Respect your host's home, belongings, privacy, and house rules.
- Communicate your arrival and departure plans clearly.
- Leave the space in reasonable condition.
- Do not invite additional guests without permission.
- Do not use the accommodation for unapproved activities.

**A shared home is not automatically an after-milonga gathering place!** Practicing tango, playing music, or inviting friends over should always be discussed with your host first.

## 4. Be Honest and Reliable

Trust is essential to a community based on sharing.

- Use your real name and provide accurate profile information.
- Describe your offers, requests, and availability honestly.
- Do not impersonate others or create misleading profiles.
- Honor confirmed arrangements whenever reasonably possible.
- If something changes, communicate promptly.
- Do not make commitments you do not intend to keep.

We understand that travel plans sometimes change. What matters is communicating clearly and treating others considerately.

## 5. Respect Privacy

Connecting with someone through Connectango does not give you permission to share their personal information.

- Do not publish or distribute another user's contact details, address, travel plans, or private communications without permission.
- Respect the confidentiality of personal conversations and arrangements.
- Use contact information obtained through Connectango only for appropriate communication related to community activities.

For more information about how Connectango handles personal data, please see our **Privacy Policy**.

## 6. Reviews and Ratings

If reviews or ratings become available on Connectango, we expect them to reflect genuine experiences.

- Share honest, fair, and respectful feedback.
- Do not submit fake or misleading reviews.
- Do not offer or accept money, discounts, favors, or other incentives in exchange for positive reviews.
- Do not use reviews to harass, retaliate against, or intimidate other members.

Feedback should help the community make informed decisions, not punish people for personal disagreements.

## 7. Safety First

Connectango helps people connect, but it does not independently verify every user's identity, background, driving record, or accommodations.

Use your own judgment when making arrangements.

We encourage you to:

- Review available profile information before confirming arrangements.
- Communicate directly with the other person about expectations.
- Meet in a public place first when practical.
- Let someone you trust know your travel plans when appropriate.
- Trust your instincts if a situation makes you uncomfortable.

**You always have the right to decline an offer or leave an uncomfortable situation.**

Your safety and well-being come first.

## 8. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited on Connectango and in arrangements made through the platform:

- Sexual harassment, assault, or unwanted physical contact
- Threats, violence, intimidation, or stalking
- Discrimination or hateful conduct
- Theft, deliberate property damage, or fraud
- Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Coercion or exploitation of other community members
- Sharing private information maliciously or without authorization
- Using Connectango to facilitate illegal activities

Serious violations may result in immediate account suspension pending review.

## 9. Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, harassment, fraud, or a safety concern connected to Connectango, please report it through the app's reporting feature or contact our support team.

We take reports seriously and will review them as appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, Connectango may issue warnings, restrict access, suspend accounts, or permanently remove users from the platform.

Serious incidents may be referred to the appropriate authorities when warranted.

**In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.** Connectango is not an emergency response service.

## 10. Building a Better Community Together

Connectango is inspired by something special about Argentine tango: wherever we travel, we can find a community.

A shared taxi might lead to a new friendship. A spare room might make it possible for someone to attend an event they otherwise couldn't afford.

These opportunities depend on people being thoughtful, reliable, and respectful.

**Share generously. Communicate clearly. Respect boundaries. Look out for each other.**

Let's make the tango world a little more connected.
